About Our Tours

Mirissa’s Premier Sea Turtle & Marine Adventure Team

Passionate local guides, ethical wildlife encounters, and unforgettable memories along Sri Lanka's south coast.

5+
Years guiding guests through Mirissa's marine world

Located on Athkam Niwasa Road in Mirissa, Mirissa Snorkeling Turtles is led by passionate local ocean guide Tharusha Shamal and a certified team of maritime guides. For over 5 years, we’ve provided travelers with authentic, safe, and ethical turtle encounters along Sri Lanka's south coast.

Whether you are an experienced swimmer or a first-time snorkeler, our small-group tours guarantee personal attention in the water. We provide premium sanitized snorkeling gear, comfortable buoyancy life jackets, and free HD/4K GoPro underwater photography & videos so your memories are captured forever.

  • 100% Wild Turtle Guarantee
  • Free GoPro 4K Photos & Videos
  • Beginner & Non-Swimmer Friendly
  • Small & Private Group Sizes
  • Complete Sanitized Gear & Jackets
  • Ethical, Eco-Friendly Encounters
